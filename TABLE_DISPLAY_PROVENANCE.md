# Table display provenance

Table_1_manuscript_display.csv and Table_2_manuscript_display.csv are exact cell exports from the final R2 English Word manuscript. They are current display exports, not reconstructed historical analyses. The preserved actual Stage06 build_documents.py contains the historical table1/table2 rendering functions and frozen displayed values; its computational and prose dependencies are retained for static inspection only. Table 1 endpoint support/count/denominator facts are traceable to Figure_1_support.csv and Table_S1_descriptive_rates.csv; primary sample and New Mexico facts are traceable to CleanF0_model_input.csv and CleanF0_NDC_source_components.csv. Table 2 model rows map separately to original audited computational scripts in model_and_figure_manifest.csv. run.py tables exports these frozen CSV displays without fitting models or running historical document generators.
